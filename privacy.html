Who we are

Digital Balance is made by Ontic, LLC. We make a $1 app for iPhone and Android that helps people build a healthier relationship with technology. That's it. We are not an ad business, not a data broker, not a SaaS company collecting behavioral signals.

What we collect

Nothing. No analytics. No tracking pixels. No cookies. No fingerprinting. No telemetry. No crash reports. No usage events. We have no idea how you use this app, and that's exactly how we want it.

Where your data lives

All data you create inside Digital Balance — your assessment answers, lesson progress, daily check-ins and exercise responses — is stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. It never leaves your device. We cannot access it, read it, or delete it. We don't have a server that receives it.

Here's a complete list of what's stored:

  • Your profile — how you answered the onboarding assessment, used to personalize your starting point
  • Lesson progress — which lessons you've completed, your quiz answers, and your scores
  • Check-ins — your daily intentionality ratings, from Autopilot to Fully intentional
  • Reminder schedule — when your optional reminders are set to appear
  • Exercise responses — your answers to the reflection exercises
  • App settings — your theme preference

Reminders are optional. If you turn them on, your phone schedules them; nothing is sent from a server.

Third-party services

None. There is no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no Mixpanel, no Sentry, no Segment, no Amplitude, no Hotjar, and no CDN that logs requests about you.

This website doesn't load anything from third parties either: its fonts are served from digitalbalance.app itself. The app bundles its fonts too, and makes no external requests of its own. Some lessons link to outside sources; opening one takes you to that site, under its own privacy policy.

Payment

If you purchased Digital Balance through the App Store, Google Play, or another payment processor, your payment was handled entirely by them under their own privacy policy. We receive confirmation that a purchase occurred — we do not receive, store, or process any payment information, card numbers, or billing details.

Exporting and deleting your data

You can export all your data as a JSON file and delete everything from within the app at any time — no account needed, no email required. The export and delete options are in the Privacy section of the app.

If you delete your data, it's gone. We have no copy to delete because we never had it.

Children

Digital Balance is not directed at children under 13. We don't collect data from anyone — including children — so COPPA doesn't apply in the usual sense. But to be clear: this program is designed for adults working through their relationship with technology.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Given that we collect nothing, meaningful changes are unlikely. We won't email you about it because we don't have your email.
