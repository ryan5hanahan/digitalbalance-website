Earning your certificate

Your certificate unlocks when you've done both of these:

  • Completed all 23 core lessons
  • Checked in on 10 different days (more than one check-in on the same day counts once)

You can see your progress toward both on the Certificate screen. Bonus lessons are optional and don't count toward it.

Reminders

The app offers optional reminders when you first set it up. Your phone schedules them; nothing is sent from a server.

To turn them off or back on, use your phone's settings: on iPhone, Settings › Notifications › Digital Balance; on Android, Settings › Apps › Digital Balance › Notifications.

Moving to a new phone

Your progress lives only on your phone, so it doesn't sync or move on its own. To bring it with you:

  • On your old phone, open Profile and tap Export backup. Save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone, such as Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email.
  • On your new phone, install Digital Balance, open Profile, tap Import backup and choose that file.

Deleting the app also deletes your progress, so export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Deleting your data

Open Profile and tap Delete all data. This erases everything on your phone and can't be undone. We have no copy, so we can't restore it for you.

Dark mode and text size

Choose Light, Dark or System under Profile › Appearance. On iPhone, the app follows your text size setting in Settings › Display & Brightness › Text Size.

Purchases and refunds

Digital Balance is a one-time purchase. To install it on another device, sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account and download it again at no charge.

Apple and Google handle all payments, so refunds go through them:

  • App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: Google Play refund help

If you need more help

Digital Balance is an educational wellness program. It isn't medical or mental-health treatment, and it isn't a substitute for care from a qualified professional.

If you're in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, in the US call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). Elsewhere, call your local emergency number or find a helpline at findahelpline.com.
